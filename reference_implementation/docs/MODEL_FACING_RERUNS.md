# Model-facing reruns

The public release contains the retained historical model-facing source together with the project-owned seeds, protocols, exact upstream row identities/hashes, pair-selection seeds, and pinned revisions needed to rebuild the manuscript inputs.

For the generic non-mode-aligned local-dynamics experiment, the recovered source is under `experiments/genericlocal/`; the canonical runner is `public_sanitized_source/scripts/run_generic_local_dynamics.py`, with historical SHA-256 `b5be93f73c40bc4c10ac375813c2e91732dea47c4158d06e18e0183357d25744`.

Historical scripts may refer to F0 captures, operator spectra, token banks, or checkpoints because those were sealed intermediate products during the original execution. In the public release these are regenerated by the preceding public preflight/materialization stages from the pinned upstream models/corpus and published selection metadata; they are not an additional private data requirement. See `reproducibility/UPSTREAM_RECONSTRUCTION.md`.

Third-party weights/corpus text remain under their original licenses and must be downloaded from their providers. Removed experiment families remain outside the active scope.
